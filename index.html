Why a Boxer, specifically

Loyal to a fault

Boxers bond hard and fast. Expect a dog that follows you room to room, not out of anxiety, but because that's where the fun is.

Built for kids

Patient, sturdy, and famously gentle with children — one of the reasons the breed keeps showing up in family homes generation after generation.

Energy with an off-switch

They play hard and then they crash. A daily walk or run keeps a Boxer easy to live with indoors.

Expressive, not subtle

Wiggle butts, head tilts, the occasional dramatic sigh. You will always know exactly how a Boxer feels about something.

Health & the practical details

Health & care

  • Vaccinated on schedule for age, first round by 8 weeks, and second round by 12 weeks.
  • Dewormed at 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks
  • Vet-checked before going home
  • 2-year genetic health guarantee
  • Includes vet records, starter food, and a care guide

Deposit & reservation

A $300 deposit holds your puppy and is applied to the final price. Deposits are transferable to a future litter but non-refundable once a puppy is assigned.

Use the Adopt Me button on any available puppy to check current availability before sending a deposit.

Pickup & delivery

  • Local pickup at our home, by appointment
  • Ground delivery available within driving range — fee based on distance
  • Flight nanny service available for longer distances, arranged on request

Questions people ask

How do I reserve a puppy?

Tap the Adopt Me button on any available puppy's card, or use the contact form below. We'll confirm availability and walk you through the $300 deposit.

What's included with a puppy?

Full vet and vaccination records, a deworming schedule, a starter bag of food, a care guide, and our 2-year genetic health guarantee.

Do you ship puppies?

We offer ground delivery within driving range and can arrange a flight nanny for longer distances. Local pickup at our home is also available by appointment.

Can I visit before deciding?

Yes — we welcome visits by appointment once a litter is old enough for guests, generally from 6 weeks.

What if the puppy I want is marked Reserved or No Longer Available?

Message us anyway — deposits occasionally fall through, and we can add you to a waitlist or point you to a sibling with a similar temperament.
